## Rules that bind, and are easy to forget

**Two taxi seats, filled DURING the draft.** A seat left empty can't be
filled mid-season. A taxi rookie carries into next season free and
consumes neither a keeper nor a drafted-rookie slot. That's why 104 and
113 go to players projected at zero — the correct use of those picks,
not a waste.

If the tool reports *"no rookie qualifies"* at those picks, that's a
filter limitation rather than an empty shelf: taxi candidates are ranked
by dynasty ADP, and the rookies without one are exactly the redshirt
population. Pick by hand and confirm each is unrostered.

**Four of your players are drafted-rookie eligible**: Brashard Smith,
Colston Loveland, Jalen Milroe, Jayden Higgins. You have two DR slots.
Dropping any of them destroys that eligibility **permanently**. Cutting
Milroe and Smith is still right, but it burns both spare options — if
Higgins busts in September there's no fallback. Decide it as a keeper
move, not roster cleanup.

**Two-stage draft.** Ten rounds now, a second draft afterwards. Finishing
under capacity is the design. Don't draft depth in rounds 8 through 10.

**Scoring is not PPR.** Tiered reception distance, first downs, half a
point per tight end catch. Volume slot receivers are worth less here
than their names suggest; tight ends more.

  measures it once picks land.

  `dynasty_value.py` for anything you intend to keep — Henry at 32 and
  Kelce at 36 look different there.
- **Depth charts move through preseason.** A Darnold injury changes
  pick 8.

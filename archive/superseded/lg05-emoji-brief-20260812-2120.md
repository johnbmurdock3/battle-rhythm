## Rules that bind

stopped naming targets.** At 102 and 115 `lineup_value` reports *"no
rookie qualifies — of 300 rookies, 117 have a dynasty price, the rest
are unrankable."* That's a limitation, not a finding: ranking taxi
candidates by dynasty ADP discards the 183 rookies without one, and
those 183 **are** the taxi population. A redshirt nobody drafts is
exactly the player no market prices. Fix it after Tuesday.

**J'Mari Taylor** (RB, JAX), **Jack Endries** (TE, CIN), **Josh Cuevas**

this year you want him active, not redshirted.

**Four players hold drafted-rookie eligibility**: Brian Thomas, Jayden
Daniels, Kyle Williams, Oronde Gadsden. Both DR slots are spent, on
Daniels and Thomas. Dropping any of the four ends that status
permanently. Cutting Williams is still right, but decide it as a keeper

**Two-stage draft.** Ten rounds, then a second draft to fill out.
Finishing under capacity is the design. Don't draft depth in rounds 8
through 10.

**Scoring is not PPR.** Tiered reception distance, first downs, half a
point per tight end catch. Volume slot receivers are worth less than
their names suggest; tight ends more.

## What this plan doesn't know

**Whether the rosters hold.** Every price assumes the 73 keepers Sleeper
shows today. All twelve teams are legal, nothing is pending a purge, but
one rival dropping a keeper before Tuesday moves everything below him.

**The other eleven draft plans.** Static market. A run on backs by the
five teams ahead of you is invisible to this.

**Marginal values are from a slot-8 run.** The ADP column is
slot-independent and correct; the point values and pick ordering are
not. Re-run.

**One season only.** `lineup_value` prices 2026. Henry at 32 and Kelce
at 36 look different through `dynasty_value.py`, and both are on this
plan on purpose.

**No concept of bye weeks.** Every swap in the plan table is me
overriding the model on a constraint it cannot score.

**Interpolated prices.** 234 of 500 players carry a real `adp_2qb`; the
rest sit on an interpolated scale. Treat those as softer.

**`room_shave` is 0 on your word, not measured.** `room_bias.py`
measures it once picks land.

**`keeper.py` reasons from the 2025 roster** and recommends Mike Evans,
whom you did not keep. Eligibility list correct, recommendations
retrospective. Its slot estimate of 6 was right and I told you to ignore
it — an estimate flagged as an estimate beat my confident guess.
